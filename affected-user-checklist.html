Affected-user checklist

Document a comparable Grok Bot failure without publishing your secrets.

Share aggregates and redacted screenshots publicly. Keep account, session, authentication, and payment identifiers private.

Record the plan and route

  • Paid plan name.
  • Identity provider.
  • Whether the SuperGrok access button appears.
  • Whether consent appears.
  • Exact error text/code.
  • UTC attempt time.

Preserve privately

  • Full callback URL.
  • Original screenshots.
  • Browser/OS versions.
  • VPN/proxy/travel context.
  • Account type and email match.
  • Prior/partial link state.
  • Support replies.

Usually safe to publish

  • Plan name and email-domain suffix.
  • Error code and visible message.
  • High-level route and dates.
  • Support outcomes.
  • Carefully redacted screenshots.

Do not publish

Complete email addresses, ticket IDs, callback/session values, cookies, access tokens, invoice/payment details, IP addresses, identity documents, or unredacted headers.

Questions to answer

  1. Did failure occur before or after consent?
  2. Did a fresh session change it?
  3. Did Cursor confirm a same-email rule?
  4. Can a new matching personal account be created?
  5. Is a permanent link present or only warned about?
  6. Did support provide a backend reason?
  7. Was the paid benefit delivered, replaced, refunded, or credited?

Comparable-report boundary

A similar report is corroborating context, not proof of a common rule. Deduplicate sources and preserve each report's limitations before aggregating users or alleged losses.
