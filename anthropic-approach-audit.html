Direct finding

The Anthropic mirror publishes no roster of journalists and no numeric journalist or press-recipient total. Its incident brief says only that privacy-sanitized tips went to “several relevant outlets.” The repository names Future Stack Reviews after independent coverage appeared; that is a coverage citation, not a recipient roster.

Private cross-check, public-safe result

A Gmail audit found 82 distinct destinations on Anthropic/Claude messages whose subject included tip during the audited campaign window. That is an email-endpoint count, not 82 individual journalists: it includes reporters, newsrooms, tips desks, and consumer-media endpoints. Three inspected threads contain human replies from correspondents at the Financial Times, Computer Weekly, and Future Stack Reviews. Addresses and private correspondence remain withheld.
