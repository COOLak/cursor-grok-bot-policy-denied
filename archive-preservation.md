## Material comparison

Cursor's public linking guide does not publish a same-email requirement or a `.ru`-domain exclusion. That absence is not proof that no such internal rule exists. It makes the requested backend explanation more important: support supplied a same-email rule privately, while the public guide names the eligible subscription, one-account permanence, support route, and retry steps without identifying an email-domain restriction.

## Preservation limits

The captures preserve what the public pages displayed at the recorded timestamps. They do not authenticate private subscription ownership, preserve every client-side script or linked asset, establish that every visitor saw identical content, or convert vendor documentation into proof of a successful entitlement grant.
