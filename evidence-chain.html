Evidence chain

What each record proves—and what it does not.

The chain preserves the route from advertised benefit to unresolved denial without turning inference into fact.

Chain of events

1. Plan representation
xAI publicly lists Grok Bot as included with SuperGrok Heavy.
2. Paid identity
Private subscription evidence and the X/xAI account establish the customer-side paid identity.
3. Cursor entry point
Grok Bot presents “Get access with SuperGrok Heavy.”
4. Authorization
The customer reaches and approves Cursor's X/xAI consent request.
5. Callback decision
Cursor's flow returns policy_denied.
6. Visible failure
Grok Bot displays “Access blocked, please contact support.”
7. Diagnostics
Requested screenshots, plan context, identity explanation, network checks, and retries are supplied.
8. Failed remediation
A reported settings refresh does not change the result.
9. Account constraint
Cursor support says emails must match and successful links are permanent.
10. Published linking terms
Cursor's public guide confirms individual Heavy eligibility, a one-account grant without a paid Cursor-plan prerequisite, permanence, and support routing, but publishes no same-email or .ru-domain restriction.
11. Corrected target
A new matching-.ru personal account is acceptable to the customer.
12. Ownership loop
xAI routes the matter to Cursor; Cursor automation closes the transferred request as duplicate.
13. Expired response window
One non-duplicated follow-up demands an owner, exact rule, supported path, ETA, or billing remedy.
14. Unresolved state
No root cause, supported path, accountable owner, ETA, or billing remedy is supplied.

Public screenshot

Proves the visible failure state beside the paid-plan path. Does not prove plan ownership, cause, link state, geography, or fault.

Private authorization screenshot

Proves the consent step and private identity context. Does not prove callback acceptance or entitlement activation.

Support correspondence

Proves what support requested and stated. Does not expose internal logs or prove behind-the-scenes work.

Still unproven

  • That .ru is the actual decision trigger.
  • Whether another account-link record exists.
  • Which company or shared service made the denial.
  • Whether the rule is intentional, erroneous, or misapplied.
  • Whether legal, compliance, fraud, regional, or technical policy is involved.

Custody model

Original private evidence remains with the customer. Public-safe artifacts receive stable paths and SHA-256 digests in evidence-manifest.json. Sensitive records are summarized, not published.
