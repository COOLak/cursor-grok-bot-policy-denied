# Evidence chain and proof boundaries

## Chain of events

1. **Plan representation** — xAI publicly lists Grok Bot as included with SuperGrok Heavy.
2. **Paid identity** — private subscription evidence and the X/xAI account establish the customer-side paid identity.
3. **Cursor entry point** — Grok Bot presents **Get access with SuperGrok Heavy**.
4. **Identity-provider authorization** — the customer reaches and approves Cursor's X/xAI consent request.
5. **Partner callback** — Cursor's flow returns `policy_denied`.
6. **Visible failure** — Grok Bot displays **“Access blocked, please contact support.”**
7. **Requested diagnostics** — screenshots, plan context, identity explanation, proxy/VPN checks, and retries are supplied.
8. **Unsuccessful remediation** — a Cursor-side settings refresh does not change the result.
9. **Account-model constraint** — Cursor states that emails must match and successful links are permanent.
10. **Corrected target requirement** — the customer confirms that a new matching-`.ru` personal Cursor account is acceptable.
11. **Ownership loop** — xAI routes the issue to Cursor; Cursor automation closes the transferred case as a duplicate.
12. **Expired response window** — one non-duplicated follow-up demands a named owner, exact evaluated rule, supported matching-`.ru` path, ETA, or billing remedy.
13. **Unresolved state** — no backend reason, supported path, accountable human owner, ETA, or billing remedy is supplied.

## What the public screenshot establishes

The screenshot establishes the visible Grok Bot access-blocked state beside the SuperGrok Heavy path. It does not establish plan ownership, backend cause, account-link state, geography, sanctions treatment, or fault.

## What the private authorization screenshot establishes

It establishes that the X/xAI consent step was reached and identifies the private X-linked address. It does not prove that Cursor accepted the callback, activated the entitlement, or evaluated the account correctly.

## What the support correspondence establishes

It records Cursor's requested diagnostics, settings-refresh claim, same-email requirement, permanent-link warning, technical-team routing, and the cross-vendor duplicate loop. It does not disclose either company's internal logs or prove what work occurred behind the support surface.

## What remains unproven

- That `.ru` is the actual decision trigger.
- Whether another account-link record exists.
- Which company or shared service made the denial decision.
- Whether the rule is intentional, erroneous, or misapplied.
- Whether a legal, compliance, fraud, regional, or technical policy is involved.

## Custody model

Original private evidence remains with the customer. Public-safe artifacts receive stable paths and SHA-256 digests in `evidence-manifest.json`. Sensitive source material is summarized, not published.
