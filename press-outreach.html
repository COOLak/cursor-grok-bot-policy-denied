Distribution policy

  • Verify current relevance and each work address from an official newsroom, staff or author page.
  • Check the private ledger, Sent mail and opt-outs before each batch; prioritize new outlets, with one destination per outlet per batch.
  • Send short, individually researched notes with explicit AI-assistance disclosure, one evidence-page link and a low-pressure question. Never use CC or BCC.
  • Keep observed failures separate from suspected causes. Do not imply a proven domain ban, legal finding or broader pattern.
  • Continue in batches of five to eight new relevant destinations every six hours toward at least 100; relevance outranks filling a quota.
  • Honor objections and opt-outs. Do not send pressure reminders, repeated initial pitches or broadcasts for count-only and formatting updates.
  • Share genuine material evidence updates once with eligible prior recipients; respond to actual questions individually.
  • Sent verification proves dispatch, not inbox delivery, readership or coverage. Record later bounces and correct counts.
