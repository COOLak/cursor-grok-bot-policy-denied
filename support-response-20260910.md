# September 10: Cursor replies, but the included Grok Bot benefit remains unresolved

Cursor replied in the original support case at **20:57 UTC on September 10, 2026**. This page summarizes the response; the original correspondence and account identifiers remain private.

## What Cursor said

Cursor directed the customer to the existing Google/Gmail-created Cursor login and advised against creating another login. It said the SuperGrok Heavy benefit on the different-email X/Grok identity cannot be attached to that login because the emails must match. It also said it cannot create a login under the matching `.ru` address from this ticket.

The response described the existing login as a free Cursor account, said Heavy on the other identity would not carry over, and clarified that Heavy does not add a free Ultra plan. It advised against cancelling Heavy, further signup attempts on the matching address, or entering a phone number on that screen. It requested the exact screen if Grok Bot remained inaccessible through the existing login.

## The unresolved question

The customer is seeking the **included Grok Bot usage grant**, not a free Cursor Ultra subscription or a mandatory transfer to the existing Gmail account. A new matching personal account is acceptable, as previously clarified to support. That signup path was blocked in the [September 9 phone evidence](mobile-signup-20260909.html).

[Cursor's current linking guide](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok) distinguishes a SuperGrok-linked usage grant from a Cursor plan and lists individual Heavy as eligible. [xAI's announcement](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-more-plans) also lists Heavy among qualifying plans. The absence of an Ultra plan is therefore not itself the complaint.

The new response does not explain how this customer can obtain the included grant under the account restrictions it describes. It supplies no exact backend finding, supported matching-account procedure, technical owner, remediation date, or billing remedy.
